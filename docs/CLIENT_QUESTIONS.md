# Questions for the client

Eight things the system currently has to guess at. Each one changes the profit
figures, so we would rather ask than assume.

**The three that matter most are 1, 2 and 3.** The rest can follow later.

---

## 1. When you refund a customer, does the marketplace give you its commission back?

You sell a phone for £400 on Amazon. Amazon keeps about **£33** as commission.
The customer returns it and you refund them in full.

**Do you get that £33 back?**

Please answer for each channel separately — they may well differ:

| | commission returned? |
|---|---|
| Amazon | |
| Back Market | |
| eBay | |
| OnBuy | |
| Temu | |

*Today the system assumes you do NOT get it back. If you do, every refund is
costing you around £33 less than the reports are showing.*

---

## 2. Profit % — measured against what you paid, or what you sold for?

You buy a phone at £300, sell at £400, and make **£60** after all fees.

- Measured against what you **paid**: 20%
- Measured against what you **sold it for**: 15%

Right now eBay shows 15% and the other four show 20%. Same phone, same £60 —
it just reads differently depending on the channel, so the channels cannot be
compared with each other.

**Which one do you want to see everywhere?**

---

## 3. If a fee changes, should last year's figures change with it?

Back Market's customer care fee went from £9.99 to £8.99. Today the system
applies £8.99 to **every sale ever made** — so a report printed in March would
print different numbers if you printed it again now.

**Should closed months stay exactly as they were, with a new fee counting only
from the day it actually changed?**

---

## 4. Do you ever give money back without taking the phone?

A customer is unhappy, keeps the phone, and you send them £30 to settle it.

The system currently knows only three endings: full refund, replacement, or
repair. There is nowhere to record that £30.

**Does this happen? Roughly how often?**

---

## 5. Is delivery included in the price you type in?

The Postage column is what **the courier charges you**. You have also told us
you refund customers the postage they paid.

**When a customer pays for delivery, is that money part of the Sale Price you
enter, or is it separate?**

*If it is separate, that income is not recorded anywhere in the system today.*

---

## 6. A phone sold in July, refunded in August — which month takes the hit?

Today July's sales figure drops, so July's profit changes after you have
already looked at it and moved on.

**Would you rather July stayed as it was, and the refund appeared as an August
cost instead?**

*You have already told us the return COSTS belong in August. This question is
about the sale itself.*

---

## 7. Is a returned phone ever thrown away?

The system assumes every returned phone is either repaired and resold, or sent
back to the supplier for credit.

**Is one ever written off as worthless?**

*If so, that is a real loss of a few hundred pounds per phone that nothing is
currently counting.*

---

## 8. Two fixed fees — every order, or only sometimes?

- **Back Market £8.99** customer care, charged on every order
- **eBay £0.40**, charged on every order

**Are those correct on every single order?** Or only in some cases — for
example, only when a customer actually contacts support?

# Already answered — recorded here so nothing gets asked twice

| | answer |
|---|---|
| Replacement carriage | Three journeys: out, faulty one back, replacement out |
| No stock for a replacement | Refund instead |
| The £1 "Accessories" line | The box and charger supplied with a phone |
| Which channels charge it | All five |
| Office vs SHS stock | Both charge it |
| Accessories sold on their own | No £1 — the charger IS the charger |
| Warranty | Full refund within 30 days, then free repair or replacement |
| Repair cost | Recorded per unit as the invoice arrives |
| Faulty unit | Comes back, repaired and resold where possible |
| Supplier returns | Full credit or a replacement unit; no carriage cost; credit lands same day |
| The Postage figure | What the carrier charges us |
| Which period a return's costs fall in | The period it was returned |
